Privacy Policy — Shopora

Last updated: November 2026

Shopora is a Bangladeshi online fashion store (website: shoporactg.web.app). This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • Orders: name, phone number, delivery address, ordered products, payment method (Cash on Delivery / bKash / Nagad).
  • Messenger / Instagram messages: messages you send to our Facebook Page or Instagram account, so we can answer your questions and process orders.
  • Basic technical data: cookies / device info needed for the website to work.

Why we use it

  • To deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • To reply to customer support messages (sometimes with the help of AI tools).
  • To publish product posts on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We never use your messages for advertising profiles.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) platform data is processed only as described in Meta's own terms.

How long we keep it

Order and message records are kept only as long as needed for delivery, support, returns/exchanges, and legal/accounting requirements. After that they are deleted.

How to request data deletion

If you want us to delete your personal data, contact us through any of these channels:

  • Email: myshopora.com@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: +880 1893398035
  • Facebook Page: Shopora (send us a message)
  • Instagram: @shopora.com26

Please include your name and phone number so we can find your records. We will confirm deletion within 30 days. If you installed our app (Shopora Auto Publisher) via Facebook Login, you can also remove it anytime in Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites.
